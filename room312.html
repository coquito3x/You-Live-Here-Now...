LAKEVIEW HOTEL — ROOM 312

Room 312

The television is on. It only shows static.

Do not answer when the radio speaks to you.
Rain runs the wrong way on the glass in this room.
Every door in the hotel opens onto the same hallway.
At night the lake is louder than the street.
My handwriting changes while I write this.
Stay if you must, but do not sign the guest book.

return to the street
